1. Executive Summary & Architecture Health Score

The neuro_mri_xai package is a well-structured, modular PyTorch pipeline (40 Python modules) suitable for BECITHCON 2026 reproducibility. CLI entry points are consistent, patient-level stratified splits are implemented, and recent fixes (Florence-2 task prompts, Swin partial fine-tuning, class-weighted loss, _sample_prediction in batch export) improve production readiness. Remaining risks are primarily cross-module consistency (SAM ROI policy, test-split alignment for batch XAI) and scientific validity (attention rollout likely non-functional).

  • Share _sample_prediction between pipeline.py and batch_export.py
